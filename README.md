Base64 is a simple Android app for encoding and decoding Base64. You type in your text, choose whether you want to encode or decode it, hit convert, and the result appears on screen. From there you can copy it to your clipboard in one tap. If you try to decode something that is not valid Base64 the app tells you rather than silently failing

This app requires Android 16 or higher and is only compatible with arm64-v8a devices
